South Coast Places for People

New Bedford and the South CoastMonthly meetup, usually at the Co-Creative Center on Union Street, plus walks and talks

Residents who want abundant housing, safe and easy ways to get around without a car, and lively places to gather. They hold a monthly meetup, run walk audits of streets such as Route 6, and bring in speakers on housing and transport.

Free to attend; membership from $25 a year

South Coast Walking Book Tour

The South Coast, a different town each timeAbout four walks a year

South Coast Almanac's walking book club. Each outing takes a book with a South Coast connection as the starting point for a walk through the place it describes, often with the author along. Free; some walks ask you to register.

Website ↗Meetup ↗

South Coast Girls Walk

New Bedford, Fairhaven, Mattapoisett, Fall River and beyondWeekly walks, listed on their schedule page

A free walking group for women, started by two friends who wanted a way to walk, talk and try the area's coffee shops together. It has grown into a region-wide community with charity walks and spin-offs such as South Coast Girls Night.

Free; just turn up

FR•NB Artist Collaborative

Fall River and New BedfordOpen calls and exhibitions through the year

Artists from the two cities working together, founded by photographers Nathaniel Roberts and Kaylin Pacheco. Open calls and group exhibitions, such as The City Of... Past, Present, Future Fall River, open to any medium.
